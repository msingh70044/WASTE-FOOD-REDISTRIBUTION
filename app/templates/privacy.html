🔒 Privacy Policy

At Share Bite, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

📋 Information We Collect

We may collect personal information from users when they register, log in, or interact with our services. This may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organization details (for NGOs and restaurants)
  • Location information

🎯 How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used in the following ways:

  • To connect NGOs with restaurants that have excess food.
  • To improve our application and services.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or services.

🔐 Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

📝 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you about significant changes in the way we treat personal information by sending a notice to the primary email address specified in your account or by placing a prominent notice on our site.

📧 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
