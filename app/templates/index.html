🍽️ Welcome to Share Bite

Share Bite is a community-driven platform that connects restaurants with excess food to NGOs and communities in need. Our mission is to reduce food waste while helping those who need it most.

🚀 How It Works

🏪 Restaurants

Post your excess food items for NGOs to collect.

🤝 NGOs

Receive food donations and mark orders as completed.

💝 Fund Donors

Support our mission with financial contributions.

📊 Admin

Monitor all activities and generate reports.

📦 Categories of Food

🍎 Fruits

Fresh and excess fruits available for donation.

🥬 Vegetables

Fresh vegetables available for donation.

🍲 Prepared Meals

Excess prepared meals available for donation.

🥖 Baked Goods

Fresh baked goods available for donation.
